# Same Stop, Different Outcome — Game Plan

## The Story
72+ million traffic stops across 4+ states. Black drivers are searched at 2x the rate of White drivers — but when searched, contraband is found LESS often. This is the statistical fingerprint of racial profiling.

## Key Stats (from 4 states: CA, FL, IL, NC)
- 72,110,871 total stops
- Black drivers searched at 1.7-2.0x the rate of White drivers
- Hit rates (contraband found when searched): White 28-73%, Black 22-55%
- Lower hit rate + higher search rate = lower threshold of suspicion for Black drivers
